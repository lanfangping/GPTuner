Set `max_wal_size` to roughly the amount of WAL you’re willing to accumulate between checkpoints—commonly **5–10% of disk space available to `pg_wal` (or a few GB on small systems)**—e.g., **2–4 GB** for light workloads, **8–32 GB** for busy OLTP, and **32–128 GB+** for heavy write/ETL, increasing it when you see checkpoints happening too frequently (e.g., **more often than ~every 5–15 minutes**) to reduce checkpoint I/O spikes.
