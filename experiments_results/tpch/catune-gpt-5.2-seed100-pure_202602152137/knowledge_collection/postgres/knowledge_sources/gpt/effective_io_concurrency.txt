Set `effective_io_concurrency` to roughly the number of concurrent asynchronous read requests your storage can actually sustain—use `0` for storage without kernel async prefetch (common on Windows), and on Linux start around **16–32 for HDD/RAID**, **32–128 for SSD/SAN**, and **128–256 (sometimes higher)** for fast NVMe/flash, then adjust based on whether increasing it continues to reduce read-heavy query latency without causing I/O saturation.
