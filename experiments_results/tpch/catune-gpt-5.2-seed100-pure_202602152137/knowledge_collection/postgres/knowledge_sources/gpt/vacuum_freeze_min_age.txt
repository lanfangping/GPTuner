Set `vacuum_freeze_min_age` to **0** for “freeze as early as possible” (common for mostly-append-only or cold tables), otherwise keep it near the default **50,000,000**; if you’re trying to reduce freeze work during aggressive vacuuming, you can raise it (often **100,000,000–200,000,000**), but don’t set it so high that it approaches `autovacuum_freeze_max_age` (default **200,000,000**)—leave a safety margin (e.g., at least **20–50 million** lower).
