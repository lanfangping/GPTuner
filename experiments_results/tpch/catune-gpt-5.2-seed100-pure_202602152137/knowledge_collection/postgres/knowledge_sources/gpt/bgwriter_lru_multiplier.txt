Set `bgwriter_lru_multiplier` based on how “spiky” your shared-buffer churn is: start at **2.0** (default), raise it in small steps (**3.0–5.0**) if you see frequent backends doing buffer writes (e.g., `buffers_backend` rising quickly or `maxwritten_clean` in `pg_stat_bgwriter` increasing), and lower it toward **1.0–1.5** only if the background writer is doing unnecessary work (stable low `buffers_backend`/`maxwritten_clean`) and you want to reduce background write activity.
