In PostgreSQL, set `backend_flush_after` to a small positive value (commonly **0** to disable, or around **64–256 8kB pages** ≈ **512KB–2MB**) to make each backend issue a writeback/flush hint after that much data is written, increasing it (or leaving **0**) when the OS already handles writeback well, and lowering it toward **64 pages** when you want steadier writeback to reduce I/O spikes.
