Set `max_parallel_maintenance_workers` to the number of CPU cores you’re willing to dedicate to a *single* maintenance operation (e.g., `VACUUM`, `CREATE INDEX`, `REINDEX`)—typically **2–4** on an **8–16 core** server, and rarely more than **8** even on larger machines—while ensuring it does not exceed `max_parallel_workers` (e.g., if `max_parallel_workers=16`, use `max_parallel_maintenance_workers=4` or `8` depending on how much foreground query capacity you want to preserve).
