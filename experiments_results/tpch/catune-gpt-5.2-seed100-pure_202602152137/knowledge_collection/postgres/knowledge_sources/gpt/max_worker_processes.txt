Set `max_worker_processes` to at least the maximum total background workers you might need at once—typically **≈ 8–16 on small servers**, and on larger servers start around **(number of CPU cores)**—e.g., if you have **16 cores** and might run **8 parallel workers** plus **3 autovacuum workers** plus **1 logical replication worker** (and a few others), set it to **≥ 12–16** (often **16 or 24**), ensuring it’s **at least `max_parallel_workers`** (and also not below your expected autovacuum/logical-replication worker needs).
