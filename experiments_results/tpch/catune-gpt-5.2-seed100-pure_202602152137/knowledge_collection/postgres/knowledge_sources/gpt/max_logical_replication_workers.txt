Set `max_logical_replication_workers` to at least the number of **concurrent logical replication tasks** you need (e.g., **#subscriptions × #tablesync workers** + **#apply workers**, often **~4–16** on small systems), ensuring it is **≤ `max_worker_processes`** (and commonly kept **≤ `max_replication_slots`**); for example, **10–20** workers is a typical starting point if you expect a handful of subscriptions with occasional initial table syncs.
