Set `autovacuum_freeze_max_age` to a high-but-safe fraction of `autovacuum_freeze_table_age` so autovacuum finishes freezing well before wraparound—typically keep the default **200,000,000** on small/medium systems, and on larger/high-churn systems raise it to about **50–80%** of `autovacuum_freeze_table_age` (default **150,000,000**), e.g., if you set `autovacuum_freeze_table_age = 1,000,000,000` then use `autovacuum_freeze_max_age ≈ 500,000,000–800,000,000`.
