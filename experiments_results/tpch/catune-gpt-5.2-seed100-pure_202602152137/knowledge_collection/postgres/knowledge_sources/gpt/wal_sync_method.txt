Set `wal_sync_method` to the fastest *durable* method supported by your storage/OS—typically leave it `fsync` (safe default), use `fdatasync` if you only need data (not metadata) synced, and for highest performance choose `open_datasync`/`open_sync` when available—then verify with `pg_test_fsync` and pick the option that gives the lowest per-sync latency (often aiming for single‑digit ms, e.g., ~0.1–5 ms on good SSDs; if you see tens of ms, e.g., 20–100 ms, your storage is the limiter and changing the method won’t fix it).
