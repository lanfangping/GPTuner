Set `max_connections` to roughly the number of **simultaneous active client sessions you truly need** (not total users), and ensure memory can cover it—rule of thumb: **if each connection costs ~5–10 MB (or more with `work_mem` usage), then 100/500/1000 connections imply ~0.5–1 GB / 2.5–5 GB / 5–10 GB+ just for connection overhead**, so in practice many deployments keep it around **100–300** and use a pooler (e.g., PgBouncer) if they need **hundreds to thousands** of client connections.
