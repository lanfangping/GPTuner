Set `autovacuum_multixact_freeze_max_age` to a “safe fraction” of `multixact_freeze_max_age` so autovacuum freezes multixacts well before wraparound—commonly **~80–95% of the hard limit** (e.g., if `multixact_freeze_max_age` is **400,000,000** default, set `autovacuum_multixact_freeze_max_age` to **320,000,000–380,000,000**), and lower it further if you see wraparound pressure or autovacuum falling behind.
