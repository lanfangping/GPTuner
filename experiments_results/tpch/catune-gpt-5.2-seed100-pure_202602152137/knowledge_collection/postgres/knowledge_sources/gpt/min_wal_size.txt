Set `min_wal_size` to roughly the amount of WAL your workload typically generates between checkpoints so Postgres doesn’t constantly force extra checkpoints to recycle space—e.g., start around **1–2× your normal checkpoint WAL volume** (commonly **1–4 GB** on moderate systems; increase to **8–32 GB** for high-write/high-throughput workloads) and raise it if you observe frequent checkpoints or “WAL file removed/recycled” churn.
