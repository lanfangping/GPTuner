Set `vacuum_multixact_freeze_min_age` to a conservative fraction of `autovacuum_multixact_freeze_max_age`—commonly **5–10%**—so tuples become eligible for multixact freezing early enough to avoid emergency wraparound work (e.g., if `autovacuum_multixact_freeze_max_age = 400,000,000`, use about **20,000,000–40,000,000**; many systems start near **5,000,000–50,000,000** depending on write churn and vacuum capacity).
