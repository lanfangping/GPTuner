Set `deadlock_timeout` to **~1 second (1000 ms)** in most cases, and tune it so it’s **well below `lock_timeout`** and roughly matches expected “normal” lock-wait durations—e.g., **500–2000 ms** for typical OLTP (lower like **200–500 ms** if you want faster deadlock detection but can tolerate more overhead/log noise; higher like **2–5 s** if long lock waits are common and deadlocks are rare).
