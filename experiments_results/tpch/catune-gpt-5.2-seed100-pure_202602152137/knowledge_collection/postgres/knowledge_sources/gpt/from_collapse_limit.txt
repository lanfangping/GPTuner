Set `from_collapse_limit` to the smallest number of `FROM` items (tables/subqueries) where you’re still okay letting the planner reorder joins for a potentially better plan—typical choices are the default **8** for general workloads, **1–4** to *reduce* planning time (less join reordering) for very join-heavy/latency-sensitive queries, or **10–20+** to *increase* optimization effort on complex multi-join queries if planning time is acceptable.
