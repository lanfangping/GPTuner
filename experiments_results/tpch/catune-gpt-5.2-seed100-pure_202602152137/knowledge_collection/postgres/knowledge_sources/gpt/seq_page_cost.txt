Set `seq_page_cost` to approximate the cost of a sequentially read page relative to `random_page_cost` (default 1.0 for SSD-like storage), e.g., keep it at **~1.0** for fast SSD/NVMe, raise it to **~1.5–3.0** for slower disks/arrays, and only consider higher values (up to **~5+**) if sequential I/O is markedly slower than random on your system—ideally calibrating by measuring sequential vs random read throughput/latency and setting the ratio accordingly.
