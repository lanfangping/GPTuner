**SENTENCE:** `wal_writer_flush_after` sets the amount of WAL generated that triggers a WAL-writer disk flush (default **1MB**); if less than this amount has been produced since the last flush and the last flush was within `wal_writer_delay`, WAL is only written to the OS page cache (not flushed), while **0** forces an immediate flush every time, and unitless values are in WAL blocks of **XLOG_BLCKSZ** (typically **8KB** each).
