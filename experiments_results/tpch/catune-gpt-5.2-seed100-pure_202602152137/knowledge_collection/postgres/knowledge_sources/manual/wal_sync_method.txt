**SENTENCE:** `wal_sync_method` selects how PostgreSQL forces WAL writes to durable storage—`open_datasync`, `fdatasync`, `fsync`, `fsync_writethrough`, or `open_sync`—and (when `fsync=on`) is applied at **each commit**; the default is the **first supported** method (typically `fdatasync` on **Linux/FreeBSD**), and it can be set **only** in `postgresql.conf` or via the **server command line**.
