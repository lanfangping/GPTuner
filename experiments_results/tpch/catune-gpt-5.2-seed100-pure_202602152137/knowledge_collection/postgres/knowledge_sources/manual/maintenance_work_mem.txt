**maintenance_work_mem** sets the per-session memory cap for maintenance tasks (e.g., VACUUM/CREATE INDEX), defaults to **64MB** (unitless values are **kB**), can be set larger than **work_mem** to speed maintenance, but note **autovacuum** can allocate up to **autovacuum_max_workers × maintenance_work_mem** total and VACUUM’s dead-tuple ID collection benefits from at most **1GB**.
