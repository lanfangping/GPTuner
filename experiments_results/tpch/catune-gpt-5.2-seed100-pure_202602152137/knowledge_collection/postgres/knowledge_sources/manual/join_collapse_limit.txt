**SENTENCE:** `join_collapse_limit` sets the maximum number of FROM items (e.g., 8) the planner may create by flattening explicit JOINs (excluding FULL JOIN) to allow join reordering—lowering it reduces planning time but can worsen plans; setting it to **1** forces the query’s written join order, and setting it to **≥ geqo_threshold** (e.g., **12**) may invoke GEQO and yield non-optimal plans.
