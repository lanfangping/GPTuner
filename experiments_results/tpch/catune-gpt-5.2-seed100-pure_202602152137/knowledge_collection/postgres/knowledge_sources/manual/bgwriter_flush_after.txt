**bgwriter_flush_after** sets how much data the background writer may write before it asks the OS to flush those writes to storage (to keep the kernel page cache from getting too dirty and to reduce fsync/checkpoint stalls), with **0 = disabled** and **max 2MB**; values without units are in **blocks of BLCKSZ (typically 8kB)**, and the default is **512kB on Linux** (**0 elsewhere**).
