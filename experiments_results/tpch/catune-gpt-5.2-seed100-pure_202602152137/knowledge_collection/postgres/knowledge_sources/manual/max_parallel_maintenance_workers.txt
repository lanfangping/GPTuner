**SENTENCE:** `max_parallel_maintenance_workers` sets how many parallel worker processes a single maintenance command (currently B-tree `CREATE INDEX` and non-`FULL` `VACUUM`) can use—default **2**, **0** disables parallelism, and the actual workers used can be fewer if constrained by `max_worker_processes`/`max_parallel_workers` (with `maintenance_work_mem` applied to the whole command, not per worker).
