**SENTENCE:** `vacuum_freeze_table_age` sets the transaction-age threshold (default **150,000,000**, configurable **0–2,000,000,000** but effectively capped at **95% of autovacuum_freeze_max_age**) at which VACUUM switches to an aggressive scan that checks every page that could contain unfrozen XIDs/MXIDs rather than only pages likely to have dead tuples.
