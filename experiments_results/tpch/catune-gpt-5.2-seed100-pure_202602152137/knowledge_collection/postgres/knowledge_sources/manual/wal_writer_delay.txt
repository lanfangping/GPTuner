**SENTENCE:** `wal_writer_delay` sets how often the WAL writer flushes WAL to disk—by default every **200ms**—sleeping for that interval after a flush unless woken by an async commit; if the last flush was < **wal_writer_delay** ago and less than **wal_writer_flush_after** WAL was generated, it only writes to the OS (no disk flush), and sleep timing is often effectively rounded to **10ms** increments.
