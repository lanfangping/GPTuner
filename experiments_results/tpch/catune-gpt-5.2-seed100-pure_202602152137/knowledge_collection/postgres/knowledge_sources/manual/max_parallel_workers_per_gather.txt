**SENTENCE:** `max_parallel_workers_per_gather` sets the per-query-node cap on parallel workers a single Gather/Gather Merge can start (default **2**; **0** disables parallel queries), subject to `max_worker_processes` and `max_parallel_workers`, and since each worker is like an extra session, using **4** workers can consume up to about **5×** the CPU/memory/I/O resources of a non-parallel run.
