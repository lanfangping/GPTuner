{'suggestion': 'Configure `wal_writer_flush_after` to control how much WAL must be generated before the WAL writer performs a disk flush: the default is `1MB`. If less than this amount has been produced since the last flush and the last flush was within `wal_writer_delay`, WAL will only be written to the OS page cache (not flushed). Setting it to `0` forces an immediate flush every time. Unitless values are interpreted in WAL blocks of `XLOG_BLCKSZ` (typically 8KB).'}
