{'suggestion': '`seq_page_cost` controls the planner’s estimated cost for each disk page read during sequential I/O (default 1.0). Tune it to reflect your storage by setting it to the approximate relative cost of sequential page reads (often ~1.0 on SSD/NVMe, potentially higher on slower disks), ideally based on measured sequential vs. random I/O performance, and override it per tablespace when specific tables/indexes reside on different storage.'}
