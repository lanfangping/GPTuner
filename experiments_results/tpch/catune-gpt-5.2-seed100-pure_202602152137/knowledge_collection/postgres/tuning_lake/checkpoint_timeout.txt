{'suggestion': 'Set `checkpoint_timeout` (maximum interval between automatic WAL checkpoints; default 5min—unitless values are in seconds) in `postgresql.conf` or via the server command line. Increasing it (often to around 10–15 minutes, or higher if appropriate) reduces checkpoint frequency and overall checkpoint-related I/O/WAL churn, but can lead to larger checkpoint I/O spikes and longer crash recovery time; mitigate spikes by tuning `checkpoint_completion_target` and background writer activity. Decrease it only if you need faster crash recovery or tighter bounds on WAL growth.'}
