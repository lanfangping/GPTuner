{'suggestion': 'Configure PostgreSQL `effective_io_concurrency` to control how many disk I/O requests it will try to run in parallel during bitmap heap scans; setting it to 0/disabled turns off async I/O (and may be appropriate on systems without kernel async prefetch, commonly Windows). On supported systems the default is typically 1. Tune it to match what your storage can actually sustain: for HDD/RAID, start around the number of physical/RAID disks (often 4–8, or roughly 16–32 for larger arrays) and adjust; for SSD/SAN you can often use higher values (about 32–128), and for fast NVMe/flash potentially 128–256 or even hundreds. Increase it gradually and keep raising it only while read-heavy latency improves without saturating I/O.'}
