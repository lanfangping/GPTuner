{'suggestion': 'Configure PostgreSQL’s `random_page_cost` to reflect the relative cost of a random (non-sequential) disk page read versus a sequential read. The default is 4.0 (based on assumptions like random I/O being much slower than sequential and many reads being cached). Lowering it—commonly into the 1.x range (e.g., ~1.1–1.2) for SSD/NVMe or when data is effectively fully cached—will make index scans more attractive to the planner, while raising it will make index scans appear more expensive.'}
