{'suggestion': 'Tune `wal_writer_flush_after` as the WAL volume threshold that makes the WAL writer fsync (flush) WAL to disk: if less than this amount (default 1MB) has been generated since the last flush and the last flush was within `wal_writer_delay`, it may only write to the OS cache. Set it lower (down to the lowest possible value) to flush every time for more continuous durability, or raise it (commonly 1–16MB, e.g., 8–16MB on high-throughput systems) to reduce flush frequency. Note that unitless values are measured in WAL blocks of 8kB each.'}
