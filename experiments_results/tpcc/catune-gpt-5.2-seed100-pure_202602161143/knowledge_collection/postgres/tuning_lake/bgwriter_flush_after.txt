{'suggestion': 'Use `bgwriter_flush_after` to limit how much data the background writer can write before forcing an OS flush, which helps cap dirty page-cache buildup and can reduce fsync/checkpoint stalls. A value of `0` disables this behavior; on Linux the default is `512kB` (and `0` elsewhere). The setting is specified in blocks of `BLCKSZ` (typically 8kB each). On Linux, consider tuning it to a small multiple of your storage writeback chunk size and iteratively adjust until bursty write I/O/latency subsides without increasing average write load.'}
