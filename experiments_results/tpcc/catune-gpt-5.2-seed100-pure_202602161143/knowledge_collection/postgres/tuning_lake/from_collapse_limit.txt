{'suggestion': 'Tune `from_collapse_limit` to control how many FROM items PostgreSQL will consider after merging subqueries into an upper query. The default is 8 and is appropriate for most workloads. Lowering it below 8 can reduce optimizer planning time (useful for very complex joins) but may lead to worse execution plans. Increasing it allows more join reordering for many-way joins, but be cautious: setting it to a value >= `geqo_threshold` can trigger GEQO, which may produce non-optimal plans.'}
