By default it is `-1`, meaning it is auto-set to `shared_buffers/32` (about 3%), clamped to 64kB–16MB. If you tune it manually, a good rule of thumb is to set it to about 3% of `shared_buffers` (e.g., `shared_buffers=512MB` -> `wal_buffers≈16MB`). The minimum effective manual value is 32kB.
