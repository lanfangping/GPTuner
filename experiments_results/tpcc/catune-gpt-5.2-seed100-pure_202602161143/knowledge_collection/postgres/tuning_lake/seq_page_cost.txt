{'suggestion': 'Set PostgreSQL `seq_page_cost` to reflect the planner’s estimated cost (in cost units) of reading one disk page during *sequential* I/O. It defaults to **1.0**, and you can override it per tablespace using the tablespace parameter **seq_page_cost**. Use **~1.0** as a practical starting point (often appropriate for fast storage like SSD/NVMe), increase it to roughly **2–4** for slower/seek-heavy storage (typical HDD), and only rarely reduce it slightly below **1.0** (e.g., **0.5–0.8**) if sequential reads are extremely cheap due to caching/mostly-in-memory workloads; then fine-tune based on whether the planner is choosing too many or too few sequential scans for your workload.'}
