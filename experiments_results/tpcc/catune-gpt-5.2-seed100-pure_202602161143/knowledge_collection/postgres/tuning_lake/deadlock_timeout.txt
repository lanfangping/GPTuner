{'suggestion': 'Set PostgreSQL `deadlock_timeout` to about 1s (the default; unitless values are milliseconds). This parameter controls how long PostgreSQL waits on a lock before running the expensive deadlock check (and, if `log_lock_waits` is enabled, before logging the wait). If your system is busy with heavy lock contention, consider increasing it (e.g., 2–10s) to reduce needless deadlock-check overhead, understanding that this will delay detection/reporting of real deadlocks.'}
