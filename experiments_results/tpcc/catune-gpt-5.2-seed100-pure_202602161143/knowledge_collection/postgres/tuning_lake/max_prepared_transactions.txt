{'suggestion': 'Configure `max_prepared_transactions` to allow the expected peak number of simultaneously **prepared** (two-phase/PREPARE TRANSACTION) transactions. This setting is **disabled by default**, must be set at **server start**, and if you use prepared transactions it is typically set to **at least `max_connections`**. On a **standby**, ensure `max_prepared_transactions` is **greater than or equal to the primary’s value** to avoid blocking queries. Keep the value no higher than necessary because higher settings consume additional shared memory.'}
