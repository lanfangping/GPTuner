{'suggestion': 'Configure `wal_sync_method` (effective only when `fsync=on`) to control which OS-level sync operation forces WAL writes to durable storage at each commit. Use the default first-supported method (typically `fdatasync` on Linux/FreeBSD) unless you have a reason to change it; if tuning, choose the method that best matches your OS/storage and delivers the lowest-latency durable flush—often `fdatasync` or `open_datasync` on ext4/XFS, reserving `open_sync` for the strongest per-write durability at a noticeable performance cost.
