{'suggestion': 'Set `max_worker_processes` to a value that can accommodate the maximum number of background worker processes you expect to run concurrently. This parameter controls the maximum number of background worker processes PostgreSQL can run and can only be changed at server start. In replication setups, ensure any standby is configured with `max_worker_processes` **greater than or equal to the primary (master)**, otherwise queries on the standby may be blocked. For sizing, you can either (a) compute it as the sum of relevant worker settings (e.g., `max_parallel_workers` + `max_parallel_maintenance_workers` plus a few for autovacuum/other extensions), or (b) use a rule of thumb of roughly 75% of CPU cores (up to ~90% on high core-count systems), while avoiding setting it higher than the available CPU cores.'}
