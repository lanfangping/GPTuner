Set `max_connections` to the smallest number that still covers your peak concurrent sessions—e.g., if you expect ~200 active client sessions at peak, set `max_connections=250–300` (to leave headroom), and ensure the memory cost fits (rule of thumb: reserve roughly **5–10 MB per connection** plus `work_mem` for active queries, so **300 connections ≈ 1.5–3 GB** baseline connection overhead before query memory).
