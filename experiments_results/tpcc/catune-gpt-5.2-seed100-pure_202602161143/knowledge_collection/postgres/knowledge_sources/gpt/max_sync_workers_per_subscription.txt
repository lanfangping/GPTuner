Set `max_sync_workers_per_subscription` to the expected peak number of tables you need to copy/initialize in parallel for a single logical replication subscription (start with **2–4**, raise to **8–16** if you have many large tables to catch up quickly), but keep it **≤ `max_logical_replication_workers`** and ensure `max_worker_processes` (and CPU/I/O capacity) can support that many extra workers.
