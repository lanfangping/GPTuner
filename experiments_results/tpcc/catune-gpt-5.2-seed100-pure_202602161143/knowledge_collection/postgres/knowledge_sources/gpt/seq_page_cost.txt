Set `seq_page_cost` to approximate the relative cost of a **sequentially read disk page** versus random I/O: keep it around **1.0** on fast storage where sequential reads are cheap (typical for **SSD/NVMe**), raise it to about **2–4** on slower/seek-heavy storage (typical **HDD**), and (rarely) lower it slightly below 1 (e.g., **0.5–0.8**) if sequential reads are extremely cheap due to **cached/mostly-in-memory** workloads; a practical starting point is **1.0** and then adjust based on whether the planner is choosing too many/few sequential scans for your workload.
