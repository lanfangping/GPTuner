Set `max_parallel_workers_per_gather` to a small integer that matches how many CPU cores you can afford per parallel query—typically **2–4** on most systems (rarely more than **8**), and ensure it also fits under the global caps `max_parallel_workers` and `max_worker_processes` (e.g., on a **16‑core** server, you might start at **4**, and only raise toward **8** if a few large queries benefit without causing CPU contention).
