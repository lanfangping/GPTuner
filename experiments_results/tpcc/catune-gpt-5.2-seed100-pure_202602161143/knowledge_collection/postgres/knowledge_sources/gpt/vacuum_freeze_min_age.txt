Set `vacuum_freeze_min_age` to a small-but-safe fraction of `autovacuum_freeze_max_age` (commonly about **5–20%**), e.g., if `autovacuum_freeze_max_age = 200,000,000` then use roughly **10,000,000–40,000,000** (often **~20,000,000**) so VACUUM can freeze tuples earlier without causing unnecessary extra freezing work.
