Set `bgwriter_delay` to the smallest value that keeps “background writer” activity smooth without causing extra CPU wakeups—typically **10–50 ms** on busy systems (default **200 ms** is often too slow for high-write workloads), and if you see frequent `buffers_backend`/`buffers_backend_fsync` in `pg_stat_bgwriter`, **decrease toward 10–20 ms**; if CPU overhead from the bgwriter is noticeable, **increase toward 100–200 ms**.
