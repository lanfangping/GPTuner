Set `bgwriter_flush_after` to a small multiple of your storage “writeback chunk” size—start at **256KB** (good general default), use **1MB** for fast SSD/NVMe, and **0** to disable (useful if your OS/storage already handles writeback well); on Linux you can align to `/proc/sys/vm/dirty_writeback_centisecs` behavior and adjust until you stop seeing bursty write I/O/latency without increasing average write load.
