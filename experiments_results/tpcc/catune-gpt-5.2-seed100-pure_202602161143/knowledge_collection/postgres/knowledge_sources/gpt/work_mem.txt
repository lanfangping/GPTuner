Set `work_mem` to about **25–50% of RAM divided by the maximum number of concurrent memory-using operations** (≈ *active sessions* × *work_mem consumers per query* such as sorts/hash joins, typically **2–5**), e.g., on a **32 GB** server with **50** active sessions and **3** consumers: target **(0.25–0.50 × 32 GB)/(50×3) ≈ 55–110 MB**, then cap it lower if you see memory pressure and raise it only for specific queries when needed.
