Set `max_wal_size` to about **2–4× the amount of WAL your system generates between checkpoints**, i.e., roughly **(WAL generation rate in MB/s) × (checkpoint_interval_seconds) × (2–4)** (for example, if you generate **10 MB/s** and checkpoint every **900 s** (15 min), start around **18–36 GB**), then adjust so checkpoints aren’t happening more often than you intend and disk space usage stays acceptable.
