Set `autovacuum_freeze_max_age` high enough to avoid wraparound emergencies but low enough that autovacuum can keep up—typically leave it at the default **200,000,000** on most systems, consider raising to **500,000,000–1,000,000,000** only if you have sufficient vacuum capacity and want less frequent aggressive freezes, and avoid going near the hard limit **2,000,000,000** since forced anti-wraparound vacuum is triggered at `autovacuum_freeze_max_age - 50,000,000` (so **150M** at default).
