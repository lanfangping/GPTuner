Set `deadlock_timeout` to **~1s (1000ms)** for most systems (PostgreSQL default) and tune it based on how often you see deadlock-related waits: **lower to 100–500ms** if you want faster deadlock detection and can tolerate extra lock-manager checks, or **raise to 2–5s** if you have very heavy lock contention and want to reduce detection overhead while accepting slower deadlock reporting.
