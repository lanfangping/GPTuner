Set `vacuum_freeze_table_age` based on how much transaction ID (XID) “headroom” you want before emergency anti-wraparound vacuum kicks in: keep it **well below `autovacuum_freeze_max_age` (default ~200,000,000)**—a common safe starting point is **~150,000,000** (≈75% of 200M), and lower it further (e.g., **50–100M**) if you want tables frozen earlier to reduce wraparound risk.
