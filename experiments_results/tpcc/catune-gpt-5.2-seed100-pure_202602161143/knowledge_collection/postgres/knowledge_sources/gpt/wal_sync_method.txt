Set `wal_sync_method` by matching it to your storage and OS for the lowest-latency durable flush: prefer `open_datasync` (common default on Linux) or `fdatasync` on ext4/XFS, use `fsync_writethrough` mainly on Windows, use `open_sync` only if you need the strongest per-write durability at a noticeable cost, and avoid `fsync` unless the others are problematic; validate by benchmarking `pg_test_fsync` and pick the method with the best “write/flush” times (often on the order of ~0.1–2 ms per sync on SSDs vs ~5–15+ ms on HDDs).
