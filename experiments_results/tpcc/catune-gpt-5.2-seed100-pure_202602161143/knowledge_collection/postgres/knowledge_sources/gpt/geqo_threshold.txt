Set `geqo_threshold` to roughly the point where PostgreSQL’s normal planner starts to struggle—keep the default **12** for typical workloads, **raise it (e.g., 15–20+)** if planning time for large multi-join queries is acceptable and you want exact plans more often, or **lower it (e.g., 8–10)** if you frequently run very large join queries and need to cap planning time by switching to GEQO earlier.
