Set `max_parallel_maintenance_workers` to the number of CPU cores you can safely devote to *maintenance* (mainly `CREATE INDEX`/`REINDEX`/`VACUUM`), typically **2–4 on small/medium servers**, and up to about **¼–½ of total cores** on larger dedicated systems—e.g., **8 cores → 2–4**, **16 cores → 4–8**, **32 cores → 8–16**—then reduce it if you see maintenance jobs saturating CPU or I/O and impacting normal query latency.
