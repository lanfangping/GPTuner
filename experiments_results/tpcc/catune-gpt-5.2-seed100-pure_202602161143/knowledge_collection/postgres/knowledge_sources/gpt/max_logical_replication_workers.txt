Set `max_logical_replication_workers` to at least the maximum number of logical replication workers you need concurrently—roughly **1 per subscription apply worker** (plus any table-sync/parallel apply workers)—so for **N subscriptions** start around **N** (e.g., **10 subscriptions → 10–15**), and increase if you use parallel apply or see “no free logical replication worker” messages (defaults **4**, max limited by `max_worker_processes`).
