Set `max_wal_senders` to the **maximum number of concurrent WAL-sender connections you expect**—i.e., **(# physical streaming replicas + # logical replication clients + # backup/WAL-archiving clients + any extra for failover/maintenance)**—and add a small buffer (commonly **+1 to +5**), for example: **0 (no replication), 3 (two standbys + one slot/backup), 6 (five standbys + one spare)**.
