**max_wal_senders** sets the maximum number of simultaneous WAL sender connections for streaming replication/base backups (**default 10; 0 disables replication**), and should be set slightly above the expected client count to tolerate orphaned slots, requires **wal_level ≥ replica**, is **startup-only**, and on a standby must be **≥ the master’s value** (or standby queries are blocked).
