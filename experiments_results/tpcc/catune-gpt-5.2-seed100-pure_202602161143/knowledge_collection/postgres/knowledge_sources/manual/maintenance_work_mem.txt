**SENTENCE:** `maintenance_work_mem` sets the per-operation memory limit for maintenance tasks (VACUUM/CREATE INDEX/ADD FOREIGN KEY), defaulting to **64MB** (unitless values are **KB**); increasing it can speed these tasks but note **autovacuum** can allocate up to **autovacuum_max_workers × maintenance_work_mem** in total, and VACUUM’s dead-tuple ID collection uses at most **1GB**.
