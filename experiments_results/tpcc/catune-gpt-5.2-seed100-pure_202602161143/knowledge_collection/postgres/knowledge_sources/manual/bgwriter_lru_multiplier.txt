**bgwriter_lru_multiplier** scales the background writer’s estimate of how many buffers will be needed next cycle—e.g., if recent demand averaged **N** new buffers per round, it targets writing enough dirty buffers to make about **N × bgwriter_lru_multiplier** reusable clean buffers (default **2.0**, “just-in-time” at **1.0**), capped at **bgwriter_lru_maxpages** writes per round.
