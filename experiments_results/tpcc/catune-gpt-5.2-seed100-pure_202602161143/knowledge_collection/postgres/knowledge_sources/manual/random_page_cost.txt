**random_page_cost** sets the planner’s cost for a *random (non-sequential) disk page read* (default **4.0**), where lowering it (e.g., **1.1** on SSDs or mostly-cached data) makes index scans more attractive and raising it makes random/index reads look more expensive (the default roughly assumes random I/O is **40×** slower than sequential but **~90%** of random reads hit cache).
