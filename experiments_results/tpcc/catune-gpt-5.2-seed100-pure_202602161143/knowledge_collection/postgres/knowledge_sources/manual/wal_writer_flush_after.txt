**SENTENCE:** `wal_writer_flush_after` sets the WAL volume threshold that triggers the WAL writer to **fsync (flush) WAL to disk**—if less than this amount (default **1MB**) has been generated since the last flush and the last flush was within `wal_writer_delay`, it may only write to the OS cache; **0** means flush every time, and unitless values are in WAL blocks of **8kB** each.
