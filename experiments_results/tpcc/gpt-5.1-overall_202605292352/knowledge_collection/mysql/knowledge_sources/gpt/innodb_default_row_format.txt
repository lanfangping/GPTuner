Set `innodb_default_row_format` to `DYNAMIC` for most workloads (it’s the default since MySQL 8.0, equivalent to specifying `ROW_FORMAT=DYNAMIC` on each table) unless you must keep legacy behavior, in which case use `COMPACT` for MySQL 5.7/8.0 or earlier versions that defaulted to `COMPACT`, and avoid `REDUNDANT` and `COMPRESSED` unless you have a specific need and have tested their impact on your data (e.g., large `TEXT/BLOB` columns or page compression).
