For `temptable_max_ram`, a practical rule is to start around **64–256 MB** on small systems and **512 MB–2 GB** on larger servers, making sure the sum of `temptable_max_ram` across all concurrent sessions (for example, `expected_concurrent_sessions × temptable_max_ram`, e.g., `100 × 64 MB = 6.4 GB`) plus other memory consumers (buffers, caches, OS) stays well below total RAM (typically keeping total MySQL memory under **60–70%** of system memory).
