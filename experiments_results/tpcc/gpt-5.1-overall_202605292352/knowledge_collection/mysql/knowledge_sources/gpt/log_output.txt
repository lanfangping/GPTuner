Set `log_output` based on how you intend to consume logs: use `'FILE'` for traditional logging to files (e.g., `/var/log/mysql/error.log` with typical rotations at 100 MB), `'TABLE'` to store logs in the `mysql.general_log` and `mysql.slow_log` tables (which can grow to gigabytes without careful `DELETE` or `TRUNCATE`), or `'FILE,TABLE'` if you explicitly require both and have capacity for roughly 2× the log volume.
