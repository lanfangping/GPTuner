Set `binlog_expire_logs_seconds` to keep only as many days of binary logs as needed for recovery and replication—for example, on a busy production system you might start with `7` days (604800 seconds), increase to `30` days (2592000 seconds) if you rely heavily on PITR or delayed replicas, or reduce to `1–2` days (86400–172800 seconds) if disk is tight and backups are frequent, always ensuring the value comfortably exceeds your longest backup + replica lag window.
