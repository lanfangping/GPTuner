Set `group_replication_consistency` to `EVENTUAL` (fastest, but may read stale data), `BEFORE_ON_PRIMARY_FAILOVER` (stronger reads only after failover), `BEFORE` (reads wait for all prior transactions to apply, trading latency for consistency), `AFTER` (commits wait for replication, typically adding a few to tens of milliseconds), or `BEFORE_AND_AFTER` (strongest consistency with both reads and writes waiting, usually the highest latency), choosing the weakest that still satisfies your application’s correctness needs.
