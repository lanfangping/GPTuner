For MySQL’s `optimizer_switch`, start from the default (e.g., MySQL 8.0: mostly `ON` with `index_merge=ON`, `index_condition_pushdown=ON`, `derived_merge=ON`, etc.) and then adjust individual flags only as needed by testing with `SET optimizer_switch='flag_name=off'` or `on` (for example, disabling `index_merge=off` or `batched_key_access=off` if you see worse plans in `EXPLAIN` or regressions in benchmarks of at least a few hundred representative queries), always comparing query latency (e.g., 95th/99th percentile) and throughput before and after over several thousand executions to confirm a statistically significant improvement.
