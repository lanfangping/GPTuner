Set `table_open_cache` to roughly `max_connections × (N + M)`—where `N` is the average number of tables used in your busiest queries (often 5–10) and `M` is a safety margin (e.g., 5–10)—then raise it gradually (e.g., to 4,000–10,000 or more on busy servers) while watching `Opened_tables` (should grow slowly) and ensuring total file descriptors needed (about `table_open_cache × 2` plus other usage) stay below the OS limit.
