Set `innodb_adaptive_hash_index` to `ON` (default) for most OLTP workloads, but consider turning it `OFF` in MySQL 5.6–5.7 if you see latch contention on the AHI in performance schema, and in MySQL 8.0 (where it’s dynamic) test both `ON` and `OFF` under production‑like load and keep the setting (1 for ON, 0 for OFF) that yields consistently lower query latency and higher throughput.
