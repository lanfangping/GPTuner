Set `innodb_flush_method` to `O_DIRECT` for dedicated MySQL servers with fast disks/SSDs and sufficient RAM (e.g., ≥16–32 GB) to avoid double-buffering, use `fsync` (the default) on smaller or shared systems or filesystems where `O_DIRECT` is unreliable, and consider `O_DIRECT_NO_FSYNC` only on very stable storage with battery-backed cache where the risk of data loss is acceptable.
