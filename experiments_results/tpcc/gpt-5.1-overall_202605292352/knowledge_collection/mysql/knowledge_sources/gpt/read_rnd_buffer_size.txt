Set `read_rnd_buffer_size` just large enough to hold the typical result set of your ORDER BY / GROUP BY queries—commonly 256KB–4MB, rarely above 8MB—by starting around 256KB and increasing in small steps (e.g., doubling up to 4MB) only if you see frequent random reads during sorted reads, while keeping the global total (`read_rnd_buffer_size` × max concurrent sessions) within available RAM.
