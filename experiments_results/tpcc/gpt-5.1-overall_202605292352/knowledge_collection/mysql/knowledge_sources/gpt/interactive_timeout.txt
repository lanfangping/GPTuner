Set `interactive_timeout` high enough to keep legitimate interactive sessions alive but low enough to reap idle connections—commonly 2,880–28,800 seconds (8 hours–8 hours workday) in traditional setups, 600–1,800 seconds (10–30 minutes) on busy/pooled systems, and as low as 60–300 seconds (1–5 minutes) for heavily loaded or security‑sensitive environments.
