Set `long_query_time` by starting at a low but practical threshold like 0.5–1.0 seconds in production (often lowered from the default 10 seconds), then iteratively reduce it in small steps (e.g., by 0.1–0.5 seconds) until the slow query log volume becomes too noisy, and finally keep it at the smallest value that still produces a manageable number of logged queries (e.g., a few hundred–few thousand per day on a busy system).
