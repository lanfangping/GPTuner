For optimal performance, enable JIT (`jit = on`) for workloads with complex queries involving many expressions or aggregations, but consider disabling it (`jit = off`) if your queries are simple or if JIT compilation overhead (typically 10-100ms) exceeds the execution time savings. JIT is most beneficial for analytical queries with complex expressions, aggregations, or large result sets, and usually benefits queries running longer than 100-200ms.
