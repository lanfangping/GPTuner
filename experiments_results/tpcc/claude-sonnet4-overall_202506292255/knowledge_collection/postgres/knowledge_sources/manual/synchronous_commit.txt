The 'synchronous_commit' knob controls how much WAL processing must complete before PostgreSQL returns success to the client, with 5 settings: 'off' (no waiting, up to 3x wal_writer_delay maximum delay), 'local' (waits for local disk flush), 'remote_write' (waits for standby to write to filesystem), 'on' (default, waits for standby to flush to durable storage), and 'remote_apply' (waits for standby to apply changes and make them visible to queries, causing the largest commit delays).
