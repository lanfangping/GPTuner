For the 'enable_nestloop' knob in PostgreSQL, set it to 'off' (false) when you want to force the query planner to avoid nested loop joins in favor of hash joins or merge joins, particularly useful when dealing with large datasets where nested loops would result in poor performance due to their O(n*m) complexity, but keep it 'on' (default true) for small tables or when you have highly selective join conditions with proper indexing.
