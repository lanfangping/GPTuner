To configure PostgreSQL's autovacuum, set `autovacuum = on` (default) in postgresql.conf and tune key parameters like `autovacuum_vacuum_scale_factor = 0.1` (triggers vacuum when 10% of table is modified), `autovacuum_vacuum_threshold = 50` (minimum 50 dead tuples), `autovacuum_max_workers = 3` (concurrent vacuum processes), and `autovacuum_naptime = 60s` (check interval), adjusting these values based on your workload intensity—decrease scale_factor to 0.05-0.02 for high-write tables and increase max_workers to 6-10 for busy systems.
