For the 'jit' knob in PostgreSQL, **enable it by setting `jit = on` for workloads with complex queries involving many expressions or aggregations, but consider disabling it (`jit = off`) if your queries are simple or if JIT compilation overhead (typically 10-100ms) exceeds the execution time savings, which usually benefits queries running longer than 100-200ms.**

The key considerations are:
- JIT compilation adds overhead of 10-100ms per query
- Most beneficial for analytical queries with complex expressions, aggregations, or large result sets
- Default is usually `on` in PostgreSQL 11+, but may hurt performance for OLTP workloads with many short queries
- Works best when combined with appropriate `jit_above_cost` settings (default 100000)
