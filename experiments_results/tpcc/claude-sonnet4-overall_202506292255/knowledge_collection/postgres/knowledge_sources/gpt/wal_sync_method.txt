For the PostgreSQL `wal_sync_method` knob, set it to `fdatasync` (default) for most systems, but consider `open_sync` or `open_datasync` if your workload has high write concurrency (>100 concurrent writers) and you're experiencing WAL write bottlenecks, as these methods can reduce context switching overhead by 20-30% on some systems, though you should benchmark both options since performance varies significantly across different storage subsystems and operating systems.
