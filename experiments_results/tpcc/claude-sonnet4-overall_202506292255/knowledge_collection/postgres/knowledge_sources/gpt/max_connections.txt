To set PostgreSQL's `max_connections`, calculate based on your available RAM using the formula: `max_connections = (RAM_in_GB - 1) * 100 / connection_memory_usage_MB`, typically resulting in values like 100-200 for small systems (4-8GB RAM), 200-500 for medium systems (16-32GB RAM), and 500-1000+ for large systems (64GB+ RAM), while ensuring each connection reserves approximately 2-5MB of memory for optimal performance.
