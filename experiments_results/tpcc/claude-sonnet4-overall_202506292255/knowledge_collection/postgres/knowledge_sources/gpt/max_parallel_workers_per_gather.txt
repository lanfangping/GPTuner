For the PostgreSQL knob 'max_parallel_workers_per_gather', set the value based on your CPU cores and workload characteristics, typically starting with 2-4 workers per gather operation (default is 2), but you can increase it up to 8-16 for CPU-intensive analytical queries on systems with many cores (16+ CPUs), while keeping it lower (1-2) for OLTP workloads to avoid context switching overhead.
