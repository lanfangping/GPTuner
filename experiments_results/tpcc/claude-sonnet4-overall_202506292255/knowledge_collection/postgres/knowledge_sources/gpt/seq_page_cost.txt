To tune PostgreSQL's `seq_page_cost` parameter, set it to reflect the relative cost of sequential disk page fetches compared to `random_page_cost`, typically using values between 0.1-1.0 for SSDs (default 1.0) and 1.0-4.0 for traditional HDDs, where a common approach is setting `seq_page_cost=0.1` and `random_page_cost=1.0` for SSDs to reflect their faster sequential access patterns.
