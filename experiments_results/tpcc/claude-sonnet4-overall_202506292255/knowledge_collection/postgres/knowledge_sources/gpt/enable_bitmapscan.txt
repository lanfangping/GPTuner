For PostgreSQL's 'enable_bitmapscan' knob, keep it at the default value 'on' unless you have tables smaller than 1000-10000 rows where bitmap scans create unnecessary overhead, or you're experiencing specific performance issues with bitmap heap scans on very large tables (>1M rows) with low selectivity queries (<1% of rows selected), in which case temporarily setting it to 'off' for testing may help determine if bitmap scans are the bottleneck.
