{'suggestion': 'Use `checkpoint_flush_after` to control how much checkpoint-written data can accumulate before PostgreSQL asks the OS to flush it, helping limit dirty page-cache buildup and reduce end-of-checkpoint fsync stalls. On Linux the default is typically 256kB (0 elsewhere), and if you omit units they default to 8kB blocks. Keep the default unless you have a specific need (e.g., direct I/O with checkpoint-write bursts); in that case try a value in the 256kB–1MB range (such as `checkpoint_flush_after = 1MB`) and increase gradually until checkpoint-related write spikes smooth out without hurting throughput.'}
