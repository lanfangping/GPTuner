{'suggestion': 'Keep `wal_log_hints` **off** (the default) unless you specifically need WAL logging of hint-bit updates (e.g., to support tools like `pg_rewind` or certain standby promotion scenarios). When enabled, PostgreSQL logs a **full-page image** to WAL the **first time a page is modified after each checkpoint**, even for hint-bit-only changes, which **increases WAL volume**. This setting is **startup-only** (requires a restart) and is **ignored if data checksums are enabled**.'}
