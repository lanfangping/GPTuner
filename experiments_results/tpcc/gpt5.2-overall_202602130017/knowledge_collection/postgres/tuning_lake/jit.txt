{'suggestion': 'The `jit` knob controls whether PostgreSQL is allowed to use JIT compilation when available; it’s a boolean setting (`on`/`off`). Enable `jit` for workloads with long-running/complex queries (e.g., queries taking ~100 ms or more) where CPU is a bottleneck, but consider disabling it for OLTP systems with many simple, short, latency-sensitive queries (e.g., <5–10 ms each) or if you observe performance regressions.'}
