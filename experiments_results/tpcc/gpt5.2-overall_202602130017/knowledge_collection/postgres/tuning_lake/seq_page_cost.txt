{'suggestion': 'Set `seq_page_cost` to control the planner’s assumed cost of reading each disk page during sequential I/O. The default is 1.0, and you can override it per tablespace via the tablespace `seq_page_cost` setting. Keep it around 1.0 for HDD-like storage, and consider lowering it (commonly ~0.2–0.5, sometimes ~0.1–0.3 on fast SSD/NVMe) when sequential scans are significantly cheaper so the planner is more likely to choose sequential scans over index access.'}
