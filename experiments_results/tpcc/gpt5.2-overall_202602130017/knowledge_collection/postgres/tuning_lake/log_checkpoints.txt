{'suggestion': 'Enable `log_checkpoints` (set it to `on`) when you want visibility into when checkpoints/restartpoints occur and how they behave. With it enabled, PostgreSQL writes a server-log entry for every checkpoint/restartpoint, including concrete statistics such as how many buffers were written and how long the writes took (e.g., in ms), which is useful for diagnosing checkpoint frequency and write I/O behavior.'}
