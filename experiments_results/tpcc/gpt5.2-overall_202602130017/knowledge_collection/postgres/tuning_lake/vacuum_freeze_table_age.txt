{'suggestion': 'Keep `vacuum_freeze_table_age` at its default (150,000,000 transactions) unless you have a clear reason and sufficient maintenance capacity to change it. When a table’s `relfrozenxid` age reaches this threshold, `VACUUM` switches to an aggressive scan that checks every page that might contain unfrozen XIDs/MXIDs. If you do raise it, do so cautiously and ensure it remains below `autovacuum_freeze_max_age` by a safe margin; note that it is effectively capped at about 95% of `autovacuum_freeze_max_age`.'}
