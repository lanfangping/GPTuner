{'suggestion': 'Use `vacuum_cost_page_miss` to control VACUUM’s estimated cost for processing a page not found in shared buffers (including buffer pool locking, hash lookup, disk read, and scanning). The default is 10 cost units. Increase it when you want VACUUM to be more throttled and reduce I/O impact on foreground queries; decrease it when you want VACUUM to be less throttled and finish faster. Tune it in conjunction with `vacuum_cost_limit` and `vacuum_cost_delay`, since the page-miss cost matters relative to those settings.'}
