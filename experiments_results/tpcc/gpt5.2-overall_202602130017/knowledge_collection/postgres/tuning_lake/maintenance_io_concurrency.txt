{'suggestion': 'Set PostgreSQL `maintenance_io_concurrency` to control how many concurrent I/O operations maintenance tasks (e.g., VACUUM/CREATE INDEX, potentially serving many sessions) may issue at once. The default is 10 on supported systems and it is disabled otherwise. Tune it to match what your storage can efficiently handle: keep it disabled on HDD/unknown storage; use roughly 32–64 for typical SSDs; and 128–256 for fast NVMe/SAN, increasing until maintenance stops getting faster and then backing off. You can also override this setting per tablespace using the tablespace parameter of the same name via `ALTER TABLESPACE`.'}
