{'suggestion': 'Configure PostgreSQL `wal_sync_method` in `postgresql.conf` (or via the server command line) to choose the OS/filesystem sync mechanism used to force WAL writes to durable storage at each commit. This setting matters only when `fsync=on`. Select among `open_datasync`, `fdatasync`, `fsync`, `fsync_writethrough`, and `open_sync` based on your OS/filesystem and storage performance; typically keep the default `fdatasync` on Linux, and consider `open_datasync` on Linux if supported by your filesystem. Validate the choice by benchmarking WAL-heavy workloads and monitoring commit latency and durability behavior.'}
