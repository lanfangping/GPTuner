{'suggestion': 'Configure `autovacuum_vacuum_insert_scale_factor` to control an insert-based autovacuum trigger as a fraction of table size: autovacuum will VACUUM when inserted tuples exceed `autovacuum_vacuum_insert_threshold + (autovacuum_vacuum_insert_scale_factor × table_size)`. The default is `0.2` (about 20% of the table; e.g., table_size=1,000,000 adds ~200,000). Tune it lower for frequently inserted or write-heavy tables (e.g., `0.05` or `0.01`) if you want earlier cleanup. Set it in `postgresql.conf`/via command line, unless overridden per-table.'}
