{'suggestion': 'Use `default_statistics_target` to control the default per-column statistics detail collected by `ANALYZE` (when no column-specific target is set). The default is 100; increase it when better planner estimates are needed—especially on larger tables or for columns heavily used in selective filters/joins. Typical tuned values are ~200 for many medium-sized systems, ~200–500 for frequently filtered/joined columns, and up to 500–1000 for large systems or highly skewed data where estimates remain poor. Increase only after confirming planner misestimates, and expect the main trade-offs to be longer `ANALYZE` times and larger statistics/metadata storage.'}
