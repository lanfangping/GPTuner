{'suggestion': 'Configure `wal_writer_flush_after` as the WAL-volume threshold that forces a disk flush (default 1MB). If less than this amount of WAL has been generated since the last flush and the last flush occurred within `wal_writer_delay`, the WAL writer will only write to the OS cache; unitless values are interpreted as WAL blocks of 8KB each. Tune it based on desired flush frequency: increase it (e.g., start around 16MB, potentially 32–64MB) to reduce flushes at the cost of more dirty WAL in cache, or decrease it (e.g., 1–8MB) to flush more frequently.'}
