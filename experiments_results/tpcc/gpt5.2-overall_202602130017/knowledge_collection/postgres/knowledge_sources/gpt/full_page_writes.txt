Set `full_page_writes` to **on** (the normal/default) whenever **WAL archiving or replication is enabled** or you need crash-safety—disabling it (**off**) only makes sense in controlled cases where you can tolerate potential page corruption after a crash and want a small write/WAL reduction; the trade-off is roughly **one extra full 8 KB page image per dirty page the first time it’s updated after each checkpoint**, so the “cost” is most noticeable with **frequent checkpoints (e.g., every 5 minutes vs 30 minutes)** and write-heavy workloads.
