Set `max_wal_size` to roughly the amount of WAL you can afford to accumulate between checkpoints—typically **~5–10% of your data directory size** or **at least 2–10× your expected WAL generation during a “checkpoint interval”**—with common starting points like **4–16 GB for small/medium systems** and **32–128 GB+ for write-heavy systems**, increasing it if you see frequent checkpoints and decreasing it if disk space or crash-recovery time becomes a concern.
