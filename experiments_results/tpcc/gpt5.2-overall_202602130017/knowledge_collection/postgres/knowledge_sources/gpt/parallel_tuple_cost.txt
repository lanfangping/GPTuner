Set `parallel_tuple_cost` to approximate the CPU time per tuple processed by a parallel worker relative to `seq_page_cost` (default `0.1`); in practice keep it around `0.05–0.2` (e.g., use `0.05–0.1` for fast CPUs/low per-tuple work to encourage parallel plans, and `0.15–0.2` if per-tuple processing is expensive or parallel overhead is high to discourage parallelism).
