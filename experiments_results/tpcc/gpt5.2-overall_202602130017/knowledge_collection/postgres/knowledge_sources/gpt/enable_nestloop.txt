Set `enable_nestloop` to `on` (default) for general OLTP workloads, but switch it to `off` only when nested-loop joins are consistently a bad choice—typically when joining large result sets without a highly selective index on the inner side (e.g., joins in the **tens/hundreds of thousands of rows** or more, where a nested loop can degenerate into **O(N×M)** work), and re-check with `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` to confirm the plan changes to hash/merge joins and reduces runtime.
