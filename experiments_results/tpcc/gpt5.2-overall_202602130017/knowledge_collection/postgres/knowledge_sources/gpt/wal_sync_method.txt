Set `wal_sync_method` to the OS/filesystem method that provides the best latency/throughput on your storage—typically keep the default `fdatasync` on Linux, prefer `open_datasync` on Linux if available for your filesystem, use `fsync_writethrough` on Windows, and validate by benchmarking WAL-heavy workloads (e.g., target stable `COMMIT` latency such as p95 < 5–10 ms) and ensuring no durability errors; the main concrete choices you’ll actually pick among are `fdatasync`, `fsync`, `open_sync`, `open_datasync`, and on Windows `fsync_writethrough` (rarely `fsync`).
