Set `checkpoint_warning` to a small value (commonly **30s–1min**, e.g., **`checkpoint_warning = '30s'`**) while tuning so Postgres logs any checkpoints happening more frequently than that—if you see warnings like “checkpoints are occurring too frequently” (e.g., every **10–20s**), increase `max_wal_size` (often to **4–16GB+**) and/or reduce checkpoint pressure until those warnings stop, then you can relax/disable the warning (set to **0**) once stable.
