Set `max_worker_processes` to at least the total number of background/parallel workers you might need at peak—typically `max_parallel_workers` + `max_parallel_maintenance_workers` + a small buffer (e.g., +2 to +5)—and keep it no higher than your available CPU capacity; for example, on a 16‑core server a common starting point is `max_parallel_workers=8`, `max_parallel_maintenance_workers=4`, so set `max_worker_processes≈14–17` (often `16`).
