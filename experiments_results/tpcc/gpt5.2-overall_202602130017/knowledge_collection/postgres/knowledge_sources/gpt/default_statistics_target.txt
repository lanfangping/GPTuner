Set `default_statistics_target` based on how “hard” your query predicates/joins are: keep the default **100** for general workloads, raise to about **200–500** for columns frequently used in selective filters/joins (and up to **1000** for very skewed distributions where estimates are still poor), but only after checking planner misestimates and remembering the trade‑off is mainly **more ANALYZE time and larger pg_statistic/metadata**, not more per-query runtime.
