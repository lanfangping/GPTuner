Set `synchronous_commit` based on how much commit latency you can tolerate versus how much data loss you can accept: use `on` (default) for full durability, use `remote_apply`/`remote_write` for synchronous replication with tighter safety (typically adds ~1–10+ ms per commit depending on network and standbys), and use `off` (or `local`) to cut commit latency (often from a few ms to sub‑ms) at the cost of losing up to about `wal_writer_delay` worth of recent transactions on a crash (default ~200 ms; so worst‑case ~0.2 s of data).
