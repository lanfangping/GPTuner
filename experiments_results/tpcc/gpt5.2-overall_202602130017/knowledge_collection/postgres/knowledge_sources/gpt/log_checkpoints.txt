Set `log_checkpoints` to `on` (it’s a boolean) in any environment where you’re diagnosing checkpoint frequency or write I/O—leave it enabled at least during tuning (so every checkpoint is logged with timing/WAL stats), and if log volume is a concern you can keep it `off` in steady-state production (roughly: `on` adds ~1 log line per checkpoint, e.g., with `checkpoint_timeout = 5min` that’s ~12 lines/hour; with `15min` that’s ~4 lines/hour).
