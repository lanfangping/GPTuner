Set `autovacuum_vacuum_insert_threshold` to roughly the number of inserts into a table you’ll tolerate before forcing a vacuum (it triggers when `inserts_since_vacuum > autovacuum_vacuum_insert_threshold + autovacuum_vacuum_insert_scale_factor * reltuples`), so a practical starting point is **1,000–10,000** for small/hot tables, **50,000–200,000** for medium tables, and **200,000–1,000,000+** for very large tables—then adjust based on observed insert-only bloat and vacuum frequency.
