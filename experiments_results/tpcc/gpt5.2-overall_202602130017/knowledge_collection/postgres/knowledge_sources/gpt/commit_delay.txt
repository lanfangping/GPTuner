Set `commit_delay` to **0 µs by default**, and only raise it (typically to **100–1000 µs**, sometimes up to **~5000–10000 µs**) when you have **many concurrent small transactions** so that multiple commits can be **grouped into one WAL flush** (usually together with `commit_siblings` around **5–10**), while verifying that the added delay doesn’t violate your latency target and that it actually improves throughput.
