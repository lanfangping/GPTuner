Set `parallel_setup_cost` to approximate the one-time overhead (in “cost units”) of starting parallel workers: keep the default **1000** if parallel plans are chosen appropriately, **raise it (e.g., 2000–10000)** if Postgres uses parallelism too often for your workload, or **lower it (e.g., 100–500)** if you want parallel plans to be chosen more aggressively (especially on systems with plenty of CPU and fast process start-up).
