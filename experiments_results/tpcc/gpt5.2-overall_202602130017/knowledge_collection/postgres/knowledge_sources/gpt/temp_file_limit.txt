Set `temp_file_limit` to a per-session cap on total temporary-file disk usage that’s safely below your free space—commonly **0 (unlimited)** on dedicated servers with ample disk, or a guardrail like **10–50 GB** on shared/production systems (e.g., if the data volume has **200 GB free**, set **50 GB**), and adjust based on observed worst-case temp usage from big sorts/aggregations and the need to avoid “disk full” incidents.
