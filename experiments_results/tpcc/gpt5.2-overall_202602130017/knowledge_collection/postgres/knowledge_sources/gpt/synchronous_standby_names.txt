Set `synchronous_standby_names` to the exact list (or count) of standby servers you want Postgres to **wait for on every commit**—e.g., `synchronous_standby_names = 'FIRST 1 (standby1)'` to require **1** specific standby, `synchronous_standby_names = 'ANY 2 (standby1,standby2,standby3)'` to require **2-of-3** standbys, or `synchronous_standby_names = ''` to require **0** (disable synchronous commit).
