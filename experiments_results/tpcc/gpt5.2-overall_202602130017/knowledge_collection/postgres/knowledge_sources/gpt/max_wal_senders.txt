Set `max_wal_senders` to the maximum number of concurrent WAL-sender processes you need—typically **equal to (or slightly above) the sum of your physical streaming replicas + logical replication clients + 1–2 headroom**, e.g., **2 standbys → 2–3**, **5 standbys + 1 logical slot → 7–8**, and if you don’t use streaming/logical replication set it to **0** (it requires a restart to change).
