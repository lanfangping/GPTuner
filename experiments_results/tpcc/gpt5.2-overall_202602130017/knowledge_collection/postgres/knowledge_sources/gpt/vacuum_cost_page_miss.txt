Set `vacuum_cost_page_miss` (default **10**) higher when you need VACUUM to be **less throttled** and finish faster (e.g., **20–50** on fast SSD/NVMe), and lower when VACUUM is causing noticeable I/O impact on foreground queries (e.g., **5–10** on busy systems or slower disks), typically evaluating the effect together with `vacuum_cost_limit` and `vacuum_cost_delay` because the miss cost only matters relative to those settings.
