Set `autovacuum_vacuum_cost_delay` by targeting a per-autovacuum-worker vacuum rate of roughly **1000–4000 cost units/second** (since rate ≈ `autovacuum_vacuum_cost_limit` / delay, e.g., **limit=2000** with **delay=20ms** gives **~100,000 cost/min ≈ 100,000/60 ≈ 1667 cost/s**); start around **10–20ms** on busy OLTP systems to reduce I/O spikes, and lower toward **0–5ms** (or **0** for “no delay”) if you need faster vacuuming and have I/O headroom.
