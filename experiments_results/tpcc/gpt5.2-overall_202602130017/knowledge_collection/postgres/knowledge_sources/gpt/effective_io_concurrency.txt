Set `effective_io_concurrency` to roughly match the number of storage requests your system can usefully keep in flight: use **0** for **HDD/network storage where read‑ahead is ineffective**, set about **1–4** for **slow/limited I/O**, around **16–32** for typical **SSD/SAN**, and **32–200** for very fast **NVMe/RAID** (increase until query latency/throughput stops improving, but don’t exceed what your storage can handle).
