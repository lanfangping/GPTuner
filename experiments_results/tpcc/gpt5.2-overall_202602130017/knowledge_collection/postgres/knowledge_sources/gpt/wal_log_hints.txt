Set `wal_log_hints` to **off (default)** unless you specifically need **page-level hint-bit WAL logging**—typically because you use **`pg_rewind`** or want the option to promote a standby after enabling **`wal_level=replica`** without taking a new base backup—in which case set it to **on** (it’s a **boolean: 0/1**, requires a restart, and increases WAL volume modestly, often on the order of **a few percent up to ~10%** depending on workload).
