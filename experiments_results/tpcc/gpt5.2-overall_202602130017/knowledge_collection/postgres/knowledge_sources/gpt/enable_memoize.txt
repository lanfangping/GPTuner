Set `enable_memoize` to `on` (the default) for most workloads, but consider turning it `off` only if `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` shows `Memoize` nodes with low hit ratios (e.g., <20–30%) and non-trivial overhead (e.g., many thousands of cache misses/evictions), since otherwise it usually reduces repeated inner scans in nested-loop joins and improves performance.
