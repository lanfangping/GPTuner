Set `bgwriter_delay` to a small interval so the background writer runs frequently—use **10–50 ms** for latency-sensitive/high-write systems (many start at **10 ms**), keep the default **200 ms** for general workloads, and increase toward **500–1000 ms** only if you’re deliberately trading smoother/less frequent background writes for lower CPU/wakeups; then validate by checking that **`pg_stat_bgwriter.checkpoints_req` stays near 0** and you aren’t seeing rising checkpoint/I/O spikes.
