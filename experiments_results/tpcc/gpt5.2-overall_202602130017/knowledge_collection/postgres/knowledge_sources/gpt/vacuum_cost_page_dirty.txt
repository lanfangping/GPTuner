Set `vacuum_cost_page_dirty` (default **20**) higher if you want autovacuum to run **less aggressively** on I/O (e.g., **40–100** to reduce impact on busy disks) and lower it if you want vacuum to **finish faster** and can tolerate more write I/O (e.g., **10–20** on SSDs or during maintenance windows), typically adjusting in tandem with `vacuum_cost_limit` / `vacuum_cost_delay` to keep overall vacuum throughput appropriate.
