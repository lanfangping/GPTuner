Set `vacuum_freeze_table_age` high enough to avoid premature aggressive anti-wraparound vacuums but low enough to ensure each table is frozen well before wraparound risk—typically leave it at the default **150,000,000** and only raise it cautiously (e.g., to **200,000,000–500,000,000**) if you have ample maintenance capacity, keeping it **below** `autovacuum_freeze_max_age` (default **200,000,000**) by a safe margin (often **≥10–20 million** transactions).
