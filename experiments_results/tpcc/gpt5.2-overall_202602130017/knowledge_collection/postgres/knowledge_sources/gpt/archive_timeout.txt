Set `archive_timeout` to a small nonzero value only if you need to guarantee WAL is archived within a fixed maximum delay: pick it as the **largest acceptable “worst‑case” archive lag** (commonly **60s–300s**, e.g., **120s**), leave it **0** if you don’t need time-based forcing, and ensure it’s **not shorter than your typical WAL segment fill time** to avoid generating lots of mostly-empty archived WAL files.
