Set `autovacuum_freeze_max_age` high enough to reduce forced anti-wraparound vacuums but low enough to avoid transaction-ID wraparound risk—keep the default **200,000,000** for most systems, and only raise it cautiously (commonly up to **~500,000,000–1,000,000,000**) if you have sufficient maintenance capacity and you also adjust related wraparound settings consistently (e.g., `vacuum_freeze_table_age` stays below it).
