Set `autovacuum` to **on** in nearly all production systems, and only turn it **off** temporarily for controlled bulk loads/maintenance (then re-enable it), because disabling it risks table/index bloat and transaction ID wraparound; if you must limit its impact instead, keep `autovacuum=on` and tune related knobs (typical starting points: `autovacuum_max_workers=3–10`, `autovacuum_naptime=10–60s`, `autovacuum_vacuum_scale_factor=0.02–0.2`, `autovacuum_analyze_scale_factor=0.02–0.1`).
