`synchronous_standby_names` defines which standby `application_name`s are eligible for synchronous replication and how many of them each commit must wait for—either the first **N** by priority (`FIRST N (s1, s2, s3, ...)`, e.g., `FIRST 3 (...)`) or **any N** as a quorum (`ANY N (...)`, e.g., `ANY 3 (...)`); if the list is empty, **0** synchronous standbys are required and commits don’t wait.
