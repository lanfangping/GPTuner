**wal_writer_delay** sets how often the WAL writer *flushes WAL to disk*—by default every **200ms**—sleeping for that interval between flushes unless woken earlier by an async commit; if the last flush was < **wal_writer_delay** ago and < **wal_writer_flush_after** WAL was generated, it may only write to the OS (not flush), and practical timing often rounds to **10ms** resolution.
