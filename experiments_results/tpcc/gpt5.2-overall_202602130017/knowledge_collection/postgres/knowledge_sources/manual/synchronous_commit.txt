`synchronous_commit` controls how far a transaction’s WAL must be persisted before PostgreSQL reports COMMIT success: `off` returns immediately (durability lag up to **3 × wal_writer_delay**), `local/on` wait for **local** WAL flush to disk, `remote_write` waits until synchronous standby **writes** WAL, `on` (default) waits until standby **flushes** WAL to durable storage, and `remote_apply` waits until standby **replays/applies** the commit (typically the highest commit latency).
