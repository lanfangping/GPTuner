**SENTENCE:** `autovacuum_work_mem` sets the per-autovacuum-worker memory limit (default **-1** = use `maintenance_work_mem`, unitless values are in **kB**), is configurable only in `postgresql.conf`/server command line, does not affect manual `VACUUM`, and is effectively capped at **1 GB** for collecting dead tuple identifiers (values above **1 GB** don’t increase what autovacuum can collect).
