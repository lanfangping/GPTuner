When a table’s `relfrozenxid` age reaches `vacuum_freeze_table_age` (default **150,000,000** transactions; user-settable **0–2,000,000,000** but effectively capped at **95% of `autovacuum_freeze_max_age`**), `VACUUM` switches to an **aggressive scan** that checks every page that might contain unfrozen XIDs/MXIDs.
