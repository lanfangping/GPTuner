**SENTENCE:** `autovacuum_vacuum_insert_scale_factor` sets the additional insert-based VACUUM trigger as a fraction of table size—VACUUM runs when inserted tuples exceed `autovacuum_vacuum_insert_threshold + (scale_factor × table_size)`, with default `0.2` meaning **20% of the table** (e.g., table_size=1,000,000 → adds **200,000**), configurable only in `postgresql.conf`/command line unless overridden per-table.
