**SENTENCE:** `wal_level` (set only at server start) controls WAL detail/volume: `minimal` writes the least (crash recovery only; no archiving/streaming replication), `replica` (default) writes enough for WAL archiving and physical replication/standby queries, and `logical` adds extra records for logical decoding and typically increases WAL volume (older `archive`/`hot_standby` map to `replica`).
